# Code of Conduct

DepthCloud welcomes people building, studying, modifying, and sharing the project, including commercial users.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss code and evidence directly; treat people respectfully.
- Offer reproducible reports and constructive technical feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, personal information, or camera captures without permission.
- Preserve attribution and applicable upstream license notices. Describe your changes and credit others' contributions honestly.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, spam, and knowingly misleading claims about results or ownership.

## Project spaces and moderation

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project-managed discussions. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Enforcement should reflect the conduct and its impact, with an explanation where practical.

For non-sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a repository issue or an existing project discussion. Do not include private evidence or personal information in a public report. A dedicated private reporting channel has not been established.
